Sunsaid Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Sunsaid is a product of Jernst Ventures LLC.

Your words stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party trackers. Your affirmations, streaks, recordings, and settings exist only on your device. The only thing Sunsaid ever sends off your phone is a small set of anonymous usage counts, described below.

Anonymous usage analytics. To understand which features are used, Sunsaid sends a small set of anonymous usage events (for example "onboarding completed" or "paywall viewed") to our own server, tagged with a random identifier created on your device. It is not connected to your identity, never includes your affirmations, your voice, or your app selections, and is never used for advertising or tracking. No third-party analytics SDKs are involved.

Microphone and speech. When you speak your affirmations, Sunsaid listens using Apple's on-device speech recognition, only while the morning screen is open. Sunsaid requires on-device recognition — audio and transcripts are never sent to any server, Apple's or ours.

Voice recordings. If you turn on "Save recordings," your cleared mornings are saved as audio files in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere. You can delete any recording, or all of them, at any time in the app.

Mirror mode. Mirror mode shows a live front-camera preview while you speak. It is a preview only — nothing is captured, recorded, or stored, and the camera session has no recording outputs.

Shielded apps. Your choice of which apps to shield is made through Apple's Family Controls picker and is opaque to Sunsaid — we never learn which apps you chose. The selection never leaves your device.

Subscriptions. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Sunsaid never sees your payment details.

Children. Sunsaid is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
